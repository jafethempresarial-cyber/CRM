## 3. Case Study Template (The "Edward" Project)
Use this when talking to potential clients. It frames you as a consultant, not a hire.

**Title: Replacing a $1,000 'Black Box' with a Secure AI Orchestrator**
*   **The Client's Problem:** The client was offered a cheap AI bot from a foreign team. It had no safety controls, no monitoring, and no human oversight.
*   **The Risk:** Brand damage, hallucinated pricing, and no way to intervene if the AI went off-track.
*   **My Solution:** I designed a custom **Enterprise CRM Orchestrator**.
    *   **Sentinel Shield:** Hard-coded guardrails to block sensitive topics.
    *   **Command Canvas:** A real-time UI for human operators to approve/edit messages.
    *   **Local-First Privacy:** Deployed on an RTX 3060 to ensure data never leaves the client's infrastructure.
*   **The Result:** The client now has the speed of AI with the safety of a human employee.
